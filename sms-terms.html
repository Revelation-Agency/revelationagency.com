Effective November 2026 · Revelation Agency, 55 Shaw Ave #201, Clovis, CA 93612

What these texts are

Revelation Agency sends text messages from its toll-free number only as notifications. These are new-lead alerts sent to the owners and staff of businesses we work for, so they can call new leads back quickly. We do not send marketing or promotional texts from this number.

Who receives them

We only text people who gave us their mobile number for this purpose. That means an owner or staff member of one of our client businesses who signed up for lead alerts on our lead alert sign-up page and checked the box agreeing to receive them.

How often

Message frequency varies with activity, usually one text per new lead. Message and data rates may apply.

To stop texts, reply STOP at any time. You will receive one confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to resume.

For help, reply HELP or call (559) 201-7039.

Your privacy

We use your mobile number only to send the notifications described above. We do not sell, rent or share mobile numbers or text-messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Phone numbers are shared only with the service providers that deliver our messages, and only for that purpose.

Carriers

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
