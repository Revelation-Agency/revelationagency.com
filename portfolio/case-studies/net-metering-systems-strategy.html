01 — Context

A System That Works While the Team Sleeps

  • ClientNet Metering Systems
  • IndustrySolar & Renewable Energy
  • Year2026
  • Live SiteNetMeteringSystems.com

The single biggest gap in most solar companies’ growth isn’t lead volume — it’s what happens after the lead comes in. Speed to contact, consistent follow-up, and intelligent sales rep coordination are where deals are won or lost. Net Metering Systems needed a back-end system that could handle this at scale without adding headcount.

GoHighLevel automations covering lead intake, follow-up, rep reminders, pipeline management, and weekly reporting.

The entire system is wired into the Revelation Portal, which connects Google and Facebook APIs for live ad performance data alongside the CRM pipeline — giving the team the intelligence to make fast decisions on follow-up timing, sales rep allocation, and pipeline health. (Search and SEO intelligence is built as a separate dedicated layer — see the SEO case study.)
